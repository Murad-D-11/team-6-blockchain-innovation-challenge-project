# Stellar Resources

<aside>

## New to Stellar?

New to Stellar? These resources will help your team get up to speed quickly. They're all drawn from Stellar's official [[Ecosystem Resources]](https://github.com/stellar/ecosystem-resources).

Bring any questions you have to the **Introduction to Stellar Workshop on Tuesday, November 3rd** and the **Technical Workshop on Tuesday, November 10th**. Recordings for both sessions will be added to the Notion following the live events.

</aside>

<aside>

## Getting Started

- [[Stellar Developer Docs]](https://developers.stellar.org/): the main reference for building on Stellar.
- [[Stellar Lab]](https://lab.stellar.org/): create test accounts and try transactions in your browser, no setup needed.
- [[Stellar Quest]](https://quest.stellar.org/): short interactive challenges for learning the basics.
- [[Guestbook Tutorial]](https://github.com/stellar/ecosystem-resources/blob/main/guestbook): build a full app on Stellar from start to finish.
</aside>

<aside>

## **Resources by problem space**

- **Payments & Remittances:** [[Infrastructure]](https://github.com/stellar/ecosystem-resources/blob/main/infrastructure) covers anchors (cash on/off-ramps), Stellar's SEP standards, and the Disbursement Platform.
- **Savings, Credit & Inflation Resilience:** [[DeFi Protocols]](https://github.com/stellar/ecosystem-resources/blob/main/defi) covers lending, yield vaults, and stablecoins. [[Tokens]](https://github.com/stellar/ecosystem-resources/blob/main/tokens) explains how assets work on Stellar.
- **Verification Infrastructure:** [[Wallet Integration]](https://github.com/stellar/ecosystem-resources/blob/main/wallet-integration) covers passkey-based smart wallets for simple, secure sign-in. [[Infrastructure]](https://github.com/stellar/ecosystem-resources/blob/main/infrastructure) explains how SEPs handle customer information between services.
</aside>

<aside>

## **For technical teams**

- [[Soroban Development]](https://github.com/stellar/ecosystem-resources/blob/main/soroban-development): smart contracts in Rust, plus testing and common pitfalls.

- [[OpenZeppelin Tools]](https://github.com/stellar/ecosystem-resources/blob/main/openzeppelin): audited contract templates and a visual Contract Wizard.
- [[Building with AI]](https://github.com/stellar/ecosystem-resources/blob/main/building-with-ai): AI coding tools, the [[Raven MCP]](https://raven.stellar.buzz/) server, and the [[Stellar Skills Marketplace]](https://skills.stellar.org/).
</aside>
