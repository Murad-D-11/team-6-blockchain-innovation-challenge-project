## Project Showcase Deliverables

**The Project Showcase is on *November 16th from 4:00-7:00 PM* in the E7 Event Space (PSE Second Floor) at the University of Waterloo.** 

**For an opportunity to be chosen as one of the Top 16 teams you must submit [this Project Showcase form](https://airtable.com/apprwC5wqwb4We3jo/pageD4iVuMUuoOvFs/form).**

---

- **A trifold board visual representation** of team’s solution. Trifold boards are available through the Velocity events team, email [velocity.events@uwaterloo.ca](mailto:velocity.events@uwaterloo.ca) if your team has not yet received one.
- **A three minute verbal presentation** delivered by one member of the team detailing the solution they have built. The presentation is required to include a one-minute demo video that demonstrates the functioning prototype. Slide-only videos do not meet this requirement. The demo video should be hosted at a publicly accessible link (e.g., YouTube or Loom) and uploaded to the Blockchain Innovation Challenge Devpost (Devpost link opens on November 6th at 12:00 PM ET).
- **Public GitHub Repository:** Teams must submit a link to a publicly accessible  [GitHub](https://github.com/) repository containing the complete project source code and a README that describes the project and provides setup and run instructions. Github repository to be uploaded to the Blockchain Innovation Challenge Devpost (Devpost link opens on November 6th at 12:00 PM ET).
- **Prototype (Live on Testnet):** Teams must deploy a functional prototype on Stellar Testnet and provide the deployed contract ID(s) and/or a live application URL so judges can verify and interact with it. Prototype link to be uploaded to the Blockchain Innovation Challenge Devpost (Devpost link opens on November 6th at 12:00 PM ET).

---

**Invite your friends and instructors to join you at the Project Showcase by sharing the public invitation link:** [https://luma.com/z6zasyza](https://luma.com/z6zasyza)

All teams will be notified by *email* to share whether they are moving on to the finals or not.

# Finalist Pitch Prep

📅 November 17th

⏰ 11:00 AM - 1:00 PM

📍Innovation Arena (280 Joseph St, Kitchener)

---

The *top eight (8) teams* will be:

- Be invited/scheduled to a *30-minute session* with experienced mentors.
- Begin with a *three (3) minute presentation*, followed by mentor feedback and advice to help strengthen your pitches for the Finals.
- *If time allows* have the opportunity to *run through your pitch again* based on the feedback received.

---
