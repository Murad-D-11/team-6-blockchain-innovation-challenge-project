## **About the challenge and what you’re building:**

**The Blockchain Innovation Challenge powered by Stellar** invites students to build social impact solutions on the Stellar network. The Stellar Development Foundation (SDF) is a non-profit dedicated to equitable access to the global financial system. Stellar is a decentralized, scalable blockchain built for financial services: transactions settle in roughly five seconds, cost a fraction of a cent, and extend through Soroban, its Rust-based smart contracts platform.

The challenge targets everyday financial services (EDFS): payments, savings, credit, insurance, and asset ownership, which remain out of reach for billions. The World Bank's 2025 Global Findex reports that 79% of adults now hold a financial account, up from 74% in 2021, yet roughly 1.3 billion adults remain unbanked globally. Women constitute about 55% of that population, and more than half come from the poorest 40% of households. Account access alone is insufficient: many holders still cannot affordably send money abroad, protect earnings from inflation, obtain credit, or invest.

Quality financial access is a strong enabler for many of the [UN Sustainable Development Goals](https://sdgs.un.org/goals). The problem spaces in the innovation challenge specifically advance SDG 1 (No Poverty), 2 (Zero Hunger), 3 (Good Health and Well-Being), 4 (Quality Education), 5 (Gender Equality), 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth), 9 (Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure), 10 (Reduced Inequalities), and 17 (Partnerships for the Goals).

Each problem space in the Blockchain Innovation Challenge names a customer and a problem rather than an architecture, and is open to teams should consider distribution, underwriting, policy, and design, in addition to software. 

Where solutions touch money movement, digital assets, or settlement, teams should build on Stellar (payments, assets, anchors and ramps, Soroban), whose speed and near-zero cost often make small-value economics viable. The strongest solutions will not ask a funder to subsidize serving underserved users; they will make serving those users structurally cheaper, so unit economics work at market prices.

## **Deliverables:**

### **For the Project Showcase (Monday, November 16th):**

- **A trifold board visual representation** of team’s solution. Trifold boards are available through the Velocity events team, email [velocity.events@uwaterloo.ca](mailto:velocity.events@uwaterloo.ca) if your team has not yet received one.
- **A three minute verbal presentation** delivered by one member of the team detailing the solution they have built. The presentation is required to include a one-minute demo video that demonstrates the functioning prototype. Slide-only videos do not meet this requirement. The demo video should be hosted at a publicly accessible link (e.g., YouTube or Loom) and [uploaded to a project on the Blockchain Innovation Challenge Devpost](https://blockchain-stellar-ic.devpost.com).
- **Prototype (Live on Testnet):** Teams must deploy a functional prototype on Stellar Testnet and provide the deployed contract ID(s) and/or a live application URL so judges can verify and interact with it. Prototype link to be uploaded to a project on the [Blockchain Innovation Challenge Devpost](https://blockchain-stellar-ic.devpost.com).

### **For the Final Presentation (top eight teams present on Wednesday, November 18th):**

- **A three minute verbal presentation accompanied by a Powerpoint slideshow** delivered by one member of the team detailing the solution they have built. The slideshow presentation is required to include a one-minute demo video that demonstrates the functioning prototype. Slide-only videos do not meet this requirement. The demo video should be hosted at a publicly accessible link (e.g., YouTube or Loom) and uploaded to a project on the [Blockchain Innovation Challenge Devpost](https://blockchain-stellar-ic.devpost.com).
- **Public GitHub Repository:** Teams must submit a link to a publicly accessible  [GitHub](https://github.com/) repository containing the complete project source code and a README that describes the project and provides setup and run instructions. Github repository to be uploaded to a project on the [Blockchain Innovation Challenge Devpost](https://blockchain-stellar-ic.devpost.com).
- **Prototype (Live on Testnet):** Teams must deploy a functional prototype on Stellar Testnet and provide the deployed contract ID(s) and/or a live application URL so judges can verify and interact with it. Prototype link to be uploaded to a project on the [Blockchain Innovation Challenge Devpost](https://blockchain-stellar-ic.devpost.com).

    **Trifold board display guidelines:** 
    
    In preparing your display, you should consider the same principles as an oral presentation but communicate it visually. Consider these three principles when communicating in your pitch or on your poster: 
    
    - Significance of details (the so what of the problem space and solution)
    - Structure of information (tell your story)
    - Simplicity (keep it simple and clean and don’t try to do or say too much)
    
    👩🏽‍🎨 **Tips for designing your trifold board:**
    Consider your main message. *Two key points* are likely all you can communicate clearly in a poster
    
    - Design your poster layout on a separate paper before preparing your final poster
    - Have someone review the design and see if they can follow the story
        - Do they think it is *visually appealing?*
        - Is the message *clear?*
        - Keep titles to about *96-point font*, headings to *30-40-point font*, other text to *24-32-point font*
        - Include bullet points, blank space, lines and high-resolution images. Keeping it visual is the key. Text should take up *no more than 25%* of the space.
    
    **⚠️ Avoid these common trifold board design mistakes:**
    
    - The objective(s) and main point(s) are hard to find or to read (paragraphs instead of points)
    - Poor organization of information that makes it difficult to follow the story
    - Too much information, messy or poor-quality graphics, difficult to read or see
