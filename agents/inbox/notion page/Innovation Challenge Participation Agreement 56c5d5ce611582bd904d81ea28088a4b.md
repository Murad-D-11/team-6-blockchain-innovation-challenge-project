# Innovation Challenge Participation Agreement

<aside>
⚠️ By participating in the Blockchain Innovation Challenge powered by Stellar, you are required to follow the terms outlined below as well as **[Velocity and the University of Waterloo’s Code of Conduct](https://www.velocityincubator.com/code-of-conduct).**

</aside>

<aside>

‼️**Please Note**
If you have *concerns* about any aspect of the Participation Agreement, email [velocity.events@uwaterloo.ca](mailto:velocity.events@uwaterloo.ca).

Velocity has the right to *remove participants* at any point if the terms outlined below or the Code of Conduct are not followed.

</aside>

<aside>

**Participation:**

- All participants must be a *current student* of the *University of Waterloo*.
- All participants are required to *attend the November 2nd Kick-Off* event and, if chosen as one of the top 16 teams, the *November 16th Project Showcase*. If you are chosen as a finalist, you must also attend the in-person *November 17th Finalist Pitch Prep* and November *18th Final* Presentations & Awards events. A full schedule of events is [linked here](Blockchain%20Innovation%20Challenge%20powered%20by%20Stellar%202245d5ce61158391aacd811206c87353.md).
- At least *one* participant from each team must *attend* all other scheduled events.
- All participants agree to be *responsive and work collaboratively* with each other throughout the duration of challenge.
- All team members agree to always conduct themselves in a *professional* and *respectful* manner.
- All participants agree to *communicate effectively* and consistently with each other and to report any issues or concerns to Velocity.

</aside>

<aside>

🫵🏽 **Dispute Resolution:**

- Any *disputes* or disagreements within the team will be handled through open and *respectful* communication and mediation.
- If a dispute cannot be resolved, team members may consult **Velocity Events** ([velocity.events@uwaterloo.ca](mailto:velocity.events@uwaterloo.ca)) from Velocity for guidance.
- Please let Velocity know as soon as an issue starts so we have enough time to resolve it.

</aside>

<aside>

**Intellectual Property:**

- All participants agree to *disclose* any existing intellectual property or *patents* that may relate to the project idea prior to the start of the challenge.
- All participants agree that any intellectual property created during the challenge is *owned jointly* by all members of the team.
- All participants agree that they will ***not*** use any intellectual property or patents they have conducted research on related to their thesis.

</aside>

<aside>

- All graduate students are required to complete the participant agreement attached below acknowledging that you are *free to pursue the commercialization* of any IP created over the *course of the Innovation Challenge*.
- You will also acknowledge that you ***should not** use any research conducted as part of your graduate studies for the Innovation Challenge*.
