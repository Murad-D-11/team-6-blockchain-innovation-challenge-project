# Solstice Alternatives Comparison

Pitch prep draft · Based on the old Laya V2 proposal.

Maria has a bill due before her first paycheck. Later, she’ll send money home. What would make her choose Solstice?

| Option | What it does well | Where it can fall short | What Solstice actually adds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ask the agency to arrange employer payment** | The agency may already have the bill and know how to reach the employer. If the employer pays the clinic directly, Maria borrows nothing. | If the employer pays her back later, she still needs money today. | It shows the employer the clinic bill and gives them a way to pay the clinic directly. The employer still has to agree. If the agency already does this, there is little to add. |
| **Ask a worker-support group** | Someone outside the agency can check who should pay and help Maria question a charge. A call or email may be enough. | Someone still has to agree to pay. | The app can flag a charge using preset rules and block financing it through Solstice. Someone still has to review or challenge the charge. The app has yet to show an advantage over a call or email. |
| **Borrow directly and send money home the usual way** | For a bill Maria owes, she can compare loan offers and keep the service her family knows. | A lender may ask for travel papers she won’t have by the time the bill is due. Paying back the loan may be a separate step. | The school confirms the bill and a lender pays the school directly. Maria can repay a share when she sends money home. Earlier funding or a lower price requires a lender to offer it. |
| **Use Solstice with a regular payment service** | A payment service can split money between family and lender. Solstice can track what Maria still owes. | Each party may rely on the service’s receipts or Solstice’s records. The service has to be able to send money to both the family and the lender. | Solstice could offer the same split with a regular service. Its receipts may be enough for everyone involved. |
| **Use Solstice with Stellar** | In the demo, Maria sends 400 in test money. Her family gets 340, her lender gets 60, and she owes 60 less. The lender can check that record without asking Solstice. | Both payments happen together only when Maria sends through Solstice. We still need to know what it costs and how her family would receive pesos. | The lender can look up the two transfers and check what Maria still owes on Stellar. The test-money record leaves real peso delivery to be confirmed separately. |

**What to show a judge:** For the same bill, compare when it gets paid and what Maria pays in total. For the same amount of pesos her family gets, compare what it costs her to send. Then show what the lender can check for itself in the Stellar demo. Real loan terms, payment costs, and who would offer the service still need testing.
