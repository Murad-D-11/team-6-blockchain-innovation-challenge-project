# Solstice research decision

November 9, 2026 · Working recommendation for the pitch team.

**Keep the contest prototype focused on the [three-step flow](../context/core-flow.md). Hold off on building a standalone service.** The demo can show who pays a bill and how one payment sends money home and reduces a loan balance. The case for a separate app depends on whether it improves the outcome compared with the [existing options](solstice-alternatives-comparison.md).

- **Bill sorting and payment.** Fair Employment Agency already covers several example expenses, including a first-timer’s TESDA assessment. Philippine rules distinguish a competency certificate from an extra assessment required by the employer. Use a fictional invoice until the team can identify a real bill, confirm who owes it, and show how Solstice could get it paid sooner or without the worker borrowing. [Fair Employment Agency](https://fairagency.org/helper-full-hire/) · [DMW rules, §§55–59](https://dmw.gov.ph/archives/v1/resources/dsms/DMW/ISN-EXT/2023/DMW-DC-01-2023.pdf)
- **Loan terms.** Budget Finance asks for a visa, ticket and Overseas Employment Certificate before releasing funds. That leaves a possible gap for earlier bills. Sri Lanka’s DFCC already ties repayment to money sent home, and PrimeCredit offers remittance and loan repayment in the same app. The useful question is whether the Solstice split could make a smaller payment count in a tight month, or lead to earlier funding or lower total cost. The demo’s 3% fee is illustrative. [Budget Finance](https://budgetfinance.ph/ofw-loan) · [DFCC](https://www.dfcc.lk/digital-banking/personal-digital-banking/ethera-saviya) · [PrimeCredit](https://www.primecredit.com/osw/primepay/) · [Fee math](research-log.md#example-break-even-math)
- **Stellar.** Have someone check both test-money transfers and the debt update in one transaction. A regular payment service can also split payments. The case for Stellar depends on whether someone needs to check the shared record independently. Compare costs using the same amount of pesos delivered to family and confirm the peso payout separately. [Contract](../../contracts/laya/src/lib.rs) · [AlipayHK corridor example](https://www.alipayhk.com/en/remittance/)

**Next check.** The research owner can use the [advisor brief](solstice-advisor-meeting.md) to pressure-test a real bill timeline and the claim that a flexible payment would matter. The build owner can verify the integrated flow on Testnet and save the transaction evidence. Treat advisor feedback, worker demand, lender terms, and demo results as separate evidence.
