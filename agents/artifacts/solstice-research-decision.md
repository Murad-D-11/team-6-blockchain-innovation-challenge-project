# Solstice research decision

November 9, 2026 · Working recommendation for the pitch team.

**Keep the contest prototype focused on the [three-step flow](../context/core-flow.md). Hold off on building a standalone service.** The demo can show who pays a bill and how one payment sends money home and reduces a loan balance. The case for a separate app depends on whether it improves the outcome compared with the [existing options](solstice-alternatives-comparison.md).

- **Bill sorting and payment.** Fair Employment Agency already covers several example expenses, including a first-timer’s TESDA assessment. Philippine rules distinguish a competency certificate from an extra assessment required by the employer. Use a fictional invoice until the team can identify a real bill, confirm who owes it, and show how Solstice could get it paid sooner or without the worker borrowing. [Fair Employment Agency](https://fairagency.org/helper-full-hire/) · [DMW rules, §§55–59](https://dmw.gov.ph/archives/v1/resources/dsms/DMW/ISN-EXT/2023/DMW-DC-01-2023.pdf)
- **Loan terms.** Budget Finance asks for a visa, ticket and Overseas Employment Certificate before releasing funds. That leaves a possible gap for earlier bills. Sri Lanka’s DFCC already ties repayment to money sent home, and PrimeCredit offers remittance and loan repayment in the same app. The useful question is whether the Solstice split could make a smaller payment count in a tight month, or lead to earlier funding or lower total cost. The demo’s 3% fee is illustrative. [Budget Finance](https://budgetfinance.ph/ofw-loan) · [DFCC](https://www.dfcc.lk/digital-banking/personal-digital-banking/ethera-saviya) · [PrimeCredit](https://www.primecredit.com/osw/primepay/) · [Fee math](research-log.md#example-break-even-math)
- **Stellar.** Have someone check both test-money transfers and the debt update in one transaction. A regular payment service can also split payments. The case for Stellar depends on whether someone needs to check the shared record independently. Compare costs using the same amount of pesos delivered to family and confirm the peso payout separately. [Contract](../../contracts/laya/src/lib.rs) · [AlipayHK corridor example](https://www.alipayhk.com/en/remittance/)

**Next check.** The research owner can use the [advisor brief](solstice-advisor-meeting.md) to pressure-test a real bill timeline and the claim that a flexible payment would matter. The build owner can verify the integrated flow on Testnet and save the transaction evidence. Treat advisor feedback, worker demand, lender terms, and demo results as separate evidence.

## Would each party use it?

These are reasons someone might participate, based on what comparable services already do. No source reviewed establishes participation in Solstice.

| Party | What they might say yes to | What could stop them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Worker | An employer-paid bill she no longer has to front, a legitimate bill paid before its deadline, or a loan that lets her pay less in a tight month without falling behind. | An extra app for the same loan and family payout. Around 59% in the updated Enrich/HKU survey already used e-wallets to send money. The survey does not measure first-time worker demand for Solstice. [Enrich/HKU](https://enrichhk.org/sites/default/files/2025-03/2024%20EnrichHK%20%26%20HKU%20SWSA%20Survey%20data_updatedMarch2025.pdf) |
| Family | The expected pesos arrive through the wallet, bank or pickup point they already use. | Less money reaches them, it arrives later, or they have to learn a new payout method. Compare the same family payout and the same amount credited to the loan. [AlipayHK](https://www.alipayhk.com/en/remittance/) |
| Employer | Pay a verified bill once and get a receipt without coordinating reimbursement. Some employers already buy packages that cover those costs. | Their agency handles it already, or they do not agree to pay earlier. [HK Labour Department guide](https://www.fdh.labour.gov.hk/res/pdf/FDHguideEnglish.pdf) · [Fair Employment Agency](https://fairagency.org/helper-full-hire/) |
| Recruitment agency | Fewer calls and fewer payment disputes, if it can handle the same case faster. | An ethical agency may already offer the service. An agency benefiting from steering workers to a lender has a reason to resist an independent review. [Fair Employment Agency](https://fairagency.org/helper-full-hire/) · [Winrock/Migrasia](https://winrock.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Asia-CTIP-Evaluating-Information-Arbitrage-and-Financial-Exploitation-by-Migration-Intermediaries.pdf) |
| Clinic or school | Get the right amount on time and match it to the right invoice. | Confirming bills in a new dashboard or handling unfamiliar payment or refund steps adds work. Willingness to accept this flow is untested. |
| Worker-support group | Review a bill independently or help a worker assemble evidence for a dispute. | A call or email may work just as well. Enrich's debt program explicitly avoids promoting financial products. [Enrich](https://enrichhk.org/debt-free-helper) |
| Lender | Verified use of funds and a repayment route that cuts servicing work or improves collection enough to offer useful terms. | Direct provider payment does not guarantee repayment. The worker can send through another app or skip a month. DFCC shows remittance repayment can exist, with a bank account and employment requirements. [DFCC](https://www.dfcc.lk/digital-banking/personal-digital-banking/ethera-saviya) |
| Money-transfer provider | Additional paid transfers or a lender relationship worth the cost of adding the split. | Existing providers already deliver to the corridor. They would need enough volume or revenue to justify added work handling payouts, support and failed payments. [AlipayHK](https://www.alipayhk.com/en/remittance/) · [PrimeCredit](https://www.primecredit.com/osw/primepay/) |

**Separate bill handling from loan distribution.** Hong Kong’s government says its employment-agency code directs agencies away from giving jobseekers personal loan information or arranging loans, including loans from overseas institutions. An agency could be relevant to employer-paid bills. Its role in distributing credit needs separate scrutiny. PrimeCredit and TranSwap already offer a closer model for the payment side, where an existing lender and transfer service work together. [Hong Kong government reply](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202610/21/P2026102100462.htm) · [TranSwap partnership](https://transwap.com/insights/transwap-introduced-as-trusted-remittance-partner-for-primecredit-leading-hong-kong-based-consumer-finance-company/)

**Best check without a lender interview.** Ask the advisor or a caseworker to replay a recent anonymized bill through the current process. Mark who was asked to pay, who actually paid, the deadline and where it got stuck. Include family or friends as an existing source of help. Then replay it with Solstice on paper. Count an advantage only if someone can name a decision or payment that changes and explain why the person responsible would agree. A separate test can compare the same family payout and loan repayment with one combined payment versus two ordinary payments. That can test convenience and price assumptions, while lender terms remain open. [Enrich first-job survey](https://enrichhk.org/id/press-release-survey-finds-nearly-30-migrant-domestic-workers-pay-1-3-months-salary-get-their-first)

## What others have tried

| Project | What changes for the worker | Evidence and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fair Employment Agency, Philippines to Hong Kong | The employer buys the hiring package. The worker avoids placement fees and the package covers specified costs such as a standard medical exam and first-time TESDA assessment. | A live recruitment service on the target route. It changes who pays through the hiring agreement. [Package](https://fairagency.org/helper-full-hire/) · [IOM case study](https://www.iom.int/sites/g/files/tmzbdl486/files/documents/2023-11/english_iom_best_practice_guidecare.pdf) |
| Issara Institute, Myanmar to Thailand | A revolving fund advances money to vetted agencies so they can cover recruitment costs while waiting for the employer to pay. Workers avoid having to front those expenses in the pilot model. | Issara reports US$48,000 allocated, 1,138 workers assisted, and 100% repayment to the fund. Operator-reported pilot results in a different corridor. [Issara program](https://www.issarainstitute.org/ethical-recruitment) |
| Good Cash, Hong Kong | A lender markets loans to foreign domestic workers as an alternative to borrowing elsewhere. | Applicants must already live in Hong Kong and hold a Hong Kong ID. Its product page establishes the offer, with no evidence there of pre-departure funding or reduced loan-shark use. [Good Cash](https://goodcash.co/) |
| EmiGrow, India to Gulf countries | The platform describes connecting workers with licensed recruiters and having a finance-company partner lend when departure costs come due. | Close product design precedent. Its public pages describe the offer and process. Borrower outcomes remain unverified in this review. [EmiGrow](https://www.emigrow.in/migration-finance.html) |
| OWWA, Philippines | The agency previously offered pre-departure loans. | OWWA said in a 2021 FOI reply that low repayments led to a halt on new applications. This describes that program at the time. [OWWA reply](https://www.foi.gov.ph/requests/ofw-pre-departure-loan-requirement/) |
| KUR Penempatan PMI, Indonesia | Subsidized bank loans cover costs for workers preparing to go abroad. | The government reported 150,561 borrowers from 2015 through March 12, 2024. That establishes reach. Reduction in loan-shark use remains unmeasured in that report. [Government report](https://www.ekon.go.id/publikasi/detail/5683/kur-penempatan-pmi-solusi-pembiayaan-bagi-calon-pekerja-migran-maupun-pekerja-magang-indonesia-di-luar-negeri) |
| Migrasia and Justice Without Borders, Philippines to Hong Kong | People help investigate abusive charges or lending and pursue a remedy across borders. | Migrasia reports complaint and enforcement activity across programs. Justice Without Borders publishes a guide to overcharging claims on the route. Neither establishes automatic refunds. [Migrasia](https://www.migrasia.org/enforcement) · [Overcharging guide](https://forjusticewithoutborders.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/Strategy-Guide-against-Overcharging-June-2023-new.pdf) |
| Impactt, several supply chains | Workers can recover recruitment fees they already paid when employers and other companies fund repayment. | Impactt reports helping facilitate over US$111 million in repayments to over 86,000 workers. These are operator-reported totals across multiple companies and sectors. [Impactt repayment work](https://impacttlimited.com/our-services/change-at-scale/principles-for-repayment-of-recruitment-fees/) |

The closest local benchmark is Fair Employment Agency. The closest challenge to Solstice's choice of borrower is Issara. If the gap exists because an employer pays too late, test whether advancing money to the party arranging the hire would keep the worker out of debt. This is a research direction from a different corridor. Check who could advance the money and who would commit to paying it back before changing the contest flow.
