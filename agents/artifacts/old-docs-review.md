# Solstice old-docs review

November 8, 2026. Recommendations for team review.

## Workflow used

1. **Extract.** Read the three PDFs in the inbox and identify what each describes: a proposed pivot, a detailed walkthrough, or a dated team plan.
2. **Compare.** Match each decision against the current pitch context, submission plan, working PRD, research log, and relevant code. Separate story examples, planned features, and proven behavior.
3. **Challenge and decide.** For each important disagreement, ask what would break if the claim were wrong, what evidence would settle it, and what to keep, revise, or leave open.

## What I would change first

1. **Business impact. Get one real bill timeline.** Ask a worker or caseworker for a recent de-identified first-time Philippines-to-Hong Kong hire. Record the bill, who owed it, deadline, when the visa and exit documents arrived, and what cheaper or employer-paid options the worker could actually use. Bring that case to a lender and ask for the total repayment, approval requirements, release date, and whether it could pay the provider. If an existing option already covers the bill, test whether routing workers to it solves the problem.
2. **Technology. Prove the whole transfer story.** On checkout `d1253bb`, provider, funder and receipt pages were present, and worker request, worker dashboard and family pages were absent. This is a checkout observation. Get the team's integrated version and run the path on Stellar's test network. Capture the school payment and the 400 → 60/340 split, the balance dropping from 195.70 to 135.70, and the public evidence. Reuse the walkthrough's failed-funding and last-payment cases as useful checks. The final payment should never take more than the remaining balance. No live run was performed for this review.
3. **Presentation. Fix wording before recording.** Avoid presenting the training bill as a required government charge, the refusal as stopping off-app abuse, or 3% as a verified real-world price. Say the app sorts the three example bills and the demo shows the loan mechanics once verified. For the historical recruitment-cost figure use roughly two months of earnings, with the 2019 survey and corridor stated. The report’s summary gives 1.8 while Table 26 gives 1.9. [ILO/PSA report](https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/2024-05/measuring-sustainable-development-goal-indicator-philippines-report-2023-en.pdf)

The historical team plan is useful for its run order and failure cases. The decision worth reopening is whether an eligible bill remains after employer payment and existing alternatives, and whether a lender can cover it by the deadline. That determines how much of the real-world pitch can rest on lending.
