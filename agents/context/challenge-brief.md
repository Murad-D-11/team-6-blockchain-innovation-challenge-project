# Solstice challenge brief

Showcase, finals, and track details below were compiled from the organizer’s Notion export. The export is absent from this checkout. The [Devpost event listing][devpost] is available for submission requirements.

## Pitch and demo requirements

- **Showcase, Top 16.** Bring a trifold display and select one teammate to give a three-minute verbal pitch at the booth. Slides are optional. The presentation “is required to include a one-minute demo video that demonstrates the functioning prototype.” The organizers specify that “Slide-only videos do not meet this requirement.”
- **Finals, Top 8.** One teammate gives “A three minute verbal presentation accompanied by a Powerpoint slideshow” including the one-minute functioning-prototype video. Three minutes of judge Q&A follow. Use the organizer’s [PowerPoint template][template] and submit in “ppt format and NO animations or transitions please and NO adjustments after submission.”
- **Devpost for both rounds.** Submit a publicly accessible demo video link, a public GitHub repo with complete source and a README with setup and run instructions, and a functional Stellar Testnet prototype with “the deployed contract ID(s) and/or a live application URL.” The live Devpost listing gives a cutoff of Nov 13 at 8:30 a.m. EST, the same as the one-page Showcase form. ## Judging criteria

| Official category | What judges look for |
| --- | --- |
| **Business Impact and Relevance (37.5%)** | Problem and social impact, solution and differentiation, customer segment, monetization, and user validation. Evidence should show the problem matters, who you serve first, how impact would be measured, how the venture could sustain itself, and what changed after testing. |
| **Technology and Implementation (37.5%)** | Meaningful and innovative use of Stellar, architecture and scalability, working and verifiable build, security and trust, and user experience and accessibility. Show an end-to-end flow and a realistic on-chain/off-chain split. |
| **Presentation & Deliverables (25%)** | Clear delivery and value proposition. For the Showcase, the organizer specifies “15% presentation and delivery and 10% effort and display quality.” |

The FAQ says “You do not need to present a revenue model,” and permits nonprofits. The rubric still scores monetization and sustainability. Prepare a credible answer on who covers operating costs, pricing and affordability, and how Solstice could scale.

## Problem space and fit

**Access to Fair Financial Assets and Affordable Financing: Savings, Credit, and Inflation Resilience.** Focus area 5 reads “Loan structures that fit irregular and seasonal income; legal enforceability across jurisdictions.” The track asks teams to explain the all-in cost, repayment structure, what happens when borrowers fall behind, and who holds the assets.

For Solstice, use the training loan and worker-selected remittance split to show the connection to irregular income. Show the employer-paid exam and the placement-fee refusal before the hypothetical training loan so viewers can see why the worker finances only one bill. The atomic split and independently inspectable payment record show what Stellar does.
