# Solstice challenge brief

November 8, 2026. Based on the organizer’s Notion export. See the [Solstice pitch context](pitch-context.md) for the team’s demo and pitch plan.

## Pitch and demo requirements

- **Showcase, Top 16.** Bring a trifold display and select one teammate to give a three-minute verbal pitch at the booth. Slides are optional. The presentation “is required to include a one-minute demo video that demonstrates the functioning prototype.” The organizers specify that “Slide-only videos do not meet this requirement.” [Deliverables][deadlines] · [Challenge schedule][challenge]
- **Finals, Top 8.** One teammate gives “A three minute verbal presentation accompanied by a Powerpoint slideshow” including the one-minute functioning-prototype video. Three minutes of judge Q&A follow. Use the organizer’s [PowerPoint template][template] and submit in “ppt format and NO animations or transitions please and NO adjustments after submission.” [FAQ][faq] · [Deliverables][deadlines]
- **Devpost for both rounds.** Submit a publicly accessible demo video link, a public GitHub repo with complete source and a README with setup and run instructions, and a functional Stellar Testnet prototype with “the deployed contract ID(s) and/or a live application URL.” The challenge overview omits the GitHub requirement from its short Showcase list, but the detailed Showcase schedule, deliverables page, and FAQ all include it. Devpost opened November 6 at noon ET. The live Devpost listing gives a cutoff of Nov 13 at 8:30 a.m. EST, the same as the one-page Showcase form. [Devpost][devpost] · [Deliverables][deadlines] · [Challenge schedule][challenge] · [FAQ][faq]

## Judging criteria

| Official category | What judges look for |
| --- | --- |
| **Business Impact and Relevance (37.5%)** | Problem and social impact, solution and differentiation, customer segment, monetization, and user validation. Evidence should show the problem matters, who you serve first, how impact would be measured, how the venture could sustain itself, and what changed after testing. |
| **Technology and Implementation (37.5%)** | Meaningful and innovative use of Stellar, architecture and scalability, working and verifiable build, security and trust, and user experience and accessibility. Show an end-to-end flow and a realistic on-chain/off-chain split. |
| **Presentation & Deliverables (25%)** | Clear delivery and value proposition. For the Showcase, the organizer specifies “15% presentation and delivery and 10% effort and display quality.” |

The FAQ says “You do not need to present a revenue model,” and permits nonprofits. The rubric still scores monetization and sustainability. Prepare a credible answer on who covers operating costs, pricing and affordability, and how Solstice could scale. [Judging criteria][judging] · [FAQ][faq]

## Problem space and fit

**Access to Fair Financial Assets and Affordable Financing: Savings, Credit, and Inflation Resilience.** Focus area 5 reads “Loan structures that fit irregular and seasonal income; legal enforceability across jurisdictions.” The track asks teams to explain the all-in cost, repayment structure, what happens when borrowers fall behind, and who holds the assets. [Problem Space #1][track]

For Solstice, use the training loan and worker-selected remittance split to show the connection to irregular income. The placement-fee refusal shows why the worker borrows less in the demo. The atomic split and independently inspectable payment record show what Stellar does. This is a pitch framing based on the published track, with team registration and any organizer confirmation still to check. [Pitch context](../context/pitch-context.md) · [Problem Space #1][track]

## Changes to review with the team

1. **Make the demo video a primary deliverable.** The current pitch context describes a backup recording and leaves live coverage open. Cut the required one-minute video from a working Testnet run and rehearse a single presenter with the video inside the three-minute slot. A separate Testnet link lets judges verify the prototype.
2. **Add the trifold and Devpost package to the plan.** The current pitch context lists the one-pager, pitch, and recording. Assign owners for the display, public video link, repo and README, and prototype URL and/or contract ID. Confirm the Showcase video playback setup with the organizer.
3. **Use the two Airtable deadlines as separate submissions.** The plan’s Nov 13 and Nov 18 times match the export. Nov 13 is the Showcase eligibility form. Nov 18 is the final deck upload for finalists. Confirm the presenter, current rehearsal evidence, and any actual user or partner feedback before writing claims.
