# Solstice pitch evidence

## Evidence that the problem exists

| Claim you can use | Source and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Recruitment costs averaged **₱49,000, roughly two months of earnings**, for Filipino domestic-services workers in Hong Kong in an analysis of 2019 survey data. | ILO and Philippine Statistics Authority, Figure 9 and executive summary. Use the rounded ratio because the summary gives 1.8 months and annex Table 26 gives 1.9. These are historical recruitment costs. Current prices, eligible loan amounts, and the share paid before the first paycheck require separate evidence. [ILO/PSA report, Figure 9](https://www.ilo.org/sites/default/files/2024-05/measuring-sustainable-development-goal-indicator-philippines-report-2023-en.pdf#page=34) |
| A worker may have to front a bill that belongs to the employer. | Hong Kong's Labour Department guide describes a process where the helper normally pays specified costs first and the employer reimburses them on receipts. The medical exam is an example. Solstice proposes direct payment to the clinic. [Hong Kong Labour Department practical guide](https://www.fdh.labour.gov.hk/res/pdf/FDHguideEnglish.pdf) |
| Philippine domestic workers are exempt from a Philippine recruitment agency's placement fee. | The Philippine DMW rules distinguish placement fees from specified worker-paid documentation and employer-paid costs. A paid training invoice needs individual verification. The demo uses a hypothetical training charge. [Philippine DMW rules, §§55–59](https://dmw.gov.ph/archives/v1/resources/dsms/DMW/ISN-EXT/2023/DMW-DC-01-2023.pdf) · [Demo bill evidence](../artifacts/solstice-demo-bill-evidence.md) |

## Evidence that narrows the product case

- **A documented case shows how the timing can play out.** ICIJ reported that a Hong Kong-bound worker was told in Manila she needed nearly US$1,200 and learned her actual monthly repayments after starting work. Use this as an example of one worker's experience. The survey above provides the broader cost estimate. [ICIJ investigation](https://www.icij.org/investigations/trafficking-inc/philippine-lenders-and-labor-agents-fleece-workers-seeking-overseas-jobs-interviews-and-confidential-documents-show/)
- **Employer-paid packages already exist.** Fair Employment Agency's Hong Kong package covers expenses such as a standard medical exam and first-time TESDA assessment. Solstice needs to identify cases that the existing hiring process leaves unresolved before claiming it reduces what the worker fronts. [Fair Employment Agency package](https://fairagency.org/helper-full-hire/)
- **Loan timing varies by provider.** Budget Finance lists a visa, ticket, and Overseas Employment Certificate among its conditions for release. This suggests a possible gap for bills due earlier. One lender's terms cannot establish that the whole market leaves that gap. [Budget Finance OFW loan](https://budgetfinance.ph/ofw-loan)
- **Remittance-linked repayment has precedent.** DFCC offers a migrant worker loan with repayment through remittances. PrimeCredit combines loan repayment and remittance services in one app. Solstice's proposed advantage depends on catching who owes the bill before lending and whether the combined payment improves the cost or experience for this corridor. [DFCC Ethera Saviya](https://www.dfcc.lk/digital-banking/personal-digital-banking/ethera-saviya) · [PrimeCredit PrimePay](https://www.primecredit.com/osw/primepay/)
- **The transfer corridor is active.** Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas reports about US$802 million in Hong Kong-attributed cash remittances from overseas Filipinos in 2024. That covers workers across sectors and reflects how banks attribute transfers. It gives context for the corridor. How often target workers remit and whether they would use a repayment split still need testing. [BSP Table 11](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/statistics/external/ofw2.aspx)

The evidence supports a real historical cost burden, different obligations by bill type, and plausible payment mechanisms. Whether the prototype would reduce borrowing, improve repayment, or deliver the same family payout at lower cost remains a question for real cases and partners.
