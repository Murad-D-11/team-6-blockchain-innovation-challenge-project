## Make the one-pager readable on its own

The pitch owner drafts this first while the build owners verify the MVP. Use the [pitch foundations](solstice-pitch-foundations.md) and [source notes](solstice-pitch-evidence.md) to draft copy for the actual Airtable fields. Give a reviewer who has never seen the project one minute to read it and ask them to explain the user, why the problem happens before payday, what the product changes, and what the prototype actually proves.

1. **Problem and user.** Start with one specific corridor and the upfront cash gap. Introduce Maria as fictional. One candidate fact: the ILO and Philippine Statistics Authority’s analysis of 2019 survey data estimated recruitment costs at roughly **two months of earnings** for domestic-services workers on the Philippines–Hong Kong corridor. Give the year and population. [ILO/PSA report and scope](solstice-pitch-evidence.md#evidence-that-the-problem-exists)
2. **Solution.** Walk through the three bills in one short paragraph. Explain who pays and when. Start with the clinic bill to show how identifying the payer can reduce borrowing. Then explain the eligible loan and how repayment works.
3. **Stellar and proof.** Explain the single remittance transaction, separate destinations and inspectable payment record. Include the deployment or demo link if the form permits it.
4. **Impact and feasibility.** Give one or two proposed measures, such as upfront amount financed by the worker or the share of bills resolved before departure. State what remains to validate and how you would test it.
