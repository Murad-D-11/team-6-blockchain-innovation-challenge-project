# How Solstice works

The demo flow starts with Maria’s bills before her first paycheck. The example uses fictional bills and test money.

1. **Sort the bills.** Maria enters her bills. Solstice uses preset rules to show which bill the employer should cover, which bill a lender could finance, and which charge the app refuses to finance.
2. **Pay the provider.** The employer can pay the clinic directly. For a bill Maria owes, the school confirms the invoice and a lender can pay the school directly.
3. **Repay while sending money home.** If Maria borrowed, part of what she sends through Solstice goes to the lender. In the example, she sends 400 in test money. Her family gets 340, the lender gets 60, and she owes 60 less. Stellar records both transfers and the updated balance.
